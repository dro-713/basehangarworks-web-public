Road Signs EE

Road Signs EE aitab sul õppida ja harjutada Eesti ja Euroopa Liidu liiklusmärke. See rakendus on mõeldud üksnes õppeotstarbeks ega ole seotud Transpordiametiga.

Road Signs EE helps you learn, practice, and look up official Estonian and EU road signs — the same signs covered in Estonian driving theory education.

Learn Mode

Browse road signs by category as flashcards. Choose "Blind" mode to test your memory before revealing the answer, or "Full View" mode to see the sign and its explanation together. Mark signs as favorites to build your own practice set.

Quiz Mode

Test yourself with multiple-choice quizzes. Choose how many questions you want (10, 20, 30, or a custom number), and quiz yourself on all signs or just your favorites.

Signs Catalogue

A complete pocket reference covering every sign category defined in Estonia's official road sign regulation — warning, priority, prohibition, mandatory, informational, and service signs, plus guide signs, supplementary plates, and road markings. Search by name or browse to find the sign you're looking for.

Works Fully Offline

No internet connection required for Learn, Quiz, or the Catalogue — study or look up a sign anywhere, including on a flight, underground, or anywhere else without signal.

Free to Try

The Priority signs category is completely free, across every mode — try the app before deciding to unlock the rest.

Full Access

Unlock every category with a single one-time purchase. Students enrolled at a partner driving school may be eligible for a reduced price — ask your school if they participate.

Available in Three Languages

Estonian, English, and Russian — switch anytime from the main menu.

Road Signs EE is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a representative of the Estonian Transport Administration (Transpordiamet) or any government entity. Sign content is based on Estonia's official traffic sign regulation, published by Riigi Teataja. The app is intended to support learning and does not guarantee passing any driving theory exam.
